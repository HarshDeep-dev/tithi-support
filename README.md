Privacy policy and support pages for **Tithi**, a free, offline Jain panchang and
Jinvani library.

Published at https://harshdeep-dev.github.io/tithi-support/

The app itself is developed in a separate repository. This one exists so that the
privacy policy and support page have a public HTTPS address, which both app stores
require. Issues here are the public place to report a wrong date or ask a question.
