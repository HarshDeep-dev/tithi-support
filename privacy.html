Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026.

Tithi does not collect your data. There is no account to make, nothing is sent to us, and the app works with every permission denied.

The short version. No accounts. No ads. No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs of any kind. No data about you leaves your phone. The calendar is computed on your device from data built into the app, so it works in airplane mode from the first launch.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

WhatWhy
Your chosen traditionTo show that tradition's calendar
Your chosen cityTo compute sunrise, sunset and pachchakkhan times where you are
Your language, and display preferencesTo show the app the way you set it
Reminders you createTo schedule them on your device
Jaap counts, and anything you markTo keep your own count between sessions

Your tradition is a religious belief. Both app stores, the GDPR and India's DPDP Act treat that as a sensitive category, and the app is built so that it never leaves your device at all — which is the only guarantee worth making about it.

Location

The app asks for location only if you tap Use my current location, and never at launch. It asks for approximate location, only while the app is open. The position is used once to find the nearest city in the list built into the app, and then discarded — only the city is kept. The app never requests background location, and it works fully if you refuse: you can pick your city from a list instead.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your phone, on your phone. No server is involved, nothing about a reminder is sent anywhere, and the app asks for notification permission only after you set a reminder — never at launch. Refusing it leaves every other part of the app working.

The network

The released app makes no network requests. It does not hold the Android internet permission.

A future version may download corrected calendar data — if an advisor rules that a date should change, that correction needs a way to reach phones between store releases. When that ships, this policy will be updated before it does, and it is designed so the request still reveals nothing: the app downloads every changed data file regardless of which tradition you follow, and your tradition, city and location are never placed in a request.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Crash information, if any, comes only from Google Play and Apple's own developer consoles, in aggregate, and never identifies you.
  • No tracking across apps or websites. The app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to ask for.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell or share.
  • No third-party content loaded at runtime. Even this page loads no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else.

Children

Tithi is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to show you, correct, export or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control: clear the app's data, or uninstall it, and it is gone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your device will be made before the app version that needs it is released.
